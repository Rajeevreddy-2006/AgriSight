## Before spraying

- Read the full product label and follow its dose, mixing and safety instructions.
- Check the weather. Do not spray if rain is expected soon, because it can wash the product off the leaves. Avoid spraying in strong wind, which carries spray drift to other fields, water and people.
- Spray in the cooler hours of the morning or evening.

## Protective clothing

Wear gloves, a mask, eye protection, long sleeves, long trousers and boots while
mixing and spraying. Do not eat, drink or smoke during spraying. Wash hands and
change clothes afterwards.

## After spraying

- Keep people and animals out of the sprayed area for the time stated on the label.
- Respect the waiting period on the label before harvest.
- Never reuse empty pesticide containers for food or water. Rinse them and dispose of them as advised by the local authority.
