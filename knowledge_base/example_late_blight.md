## Symptoms

Late blight is caused by the water mould Phytophthora infestans. Leaves show dark,
water-soaked patches that grow quickly in cool, humid or rainy weather. A white,
downy growth may appear on the underside of leaves at the edge of the patches.
Stems and fruits or tubers can also turn brown and rot.

## Cultural management

- Remove and destroy infected leaves and plants as soon as they are seen. Do not leave them in the field or on compost heaps.
- Avoid overhead irrigation. Water at the base of plants so the leaves stay dry.
- Improve air flow with proper plant spacing and staking.
- Practise crop rotation and do not grow tomato or potato in the same field season after season.
- Use certified disease-free seed and seed tubers and, where available, tolerant varieties.
- Do not save seed or tubers from infected plants for the next season.

## Chemical control

Fungicides are used preventively when weather favours the disease. The choice of
product, dose and spray interval must follow the product label and the current
recommendation of the local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agriculture officer.
